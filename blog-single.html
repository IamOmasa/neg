KIK FARMS -New generation of farms and technologies in africa

KIK Farms is an innovative horticultural production initiative that serves as both a commercial farm and a practical test lab for new technologies developed by Neg AgriTech . This farm plays a crucial role in ensuring the financial sustainability of our company’s daily operations, particularly in advancing agricultural technologies.

As KIK Farms grows with technological advancements, we envision expanding into large-scale commercial farming—eventually operating a 500+ acre farm equipped with cutting-edge smart farming technologies. This expansion will enable us to produce high-quality crops for both the export market and local value-added processing.

KiK Farms started from a challenge we faced firsthand—the lack of reliable funding for research and development in early-stage solutions. We had great ideas and technology, but without a sustainable source of funding, pushing these innovations to farmers was difficult. That’s when we decided to build a demonstration farm—a farm that not only showcases our smart agriculture solutions but also generates income to fund more research and development. — James Kabati, CEO, Neg AgriTech

Beyond production, KIK Farms will have a significant impact on technology development, community empowerment, and economic growth. It will serve as a training hub, provide access to modern agricultural technologies, create employment opportunities, and drive sustainable agricultural transformation in the region.

A Farm Beyond Agriculture

KIK Farms is not just a farm—it’s a living lab and technology hub for Africa’s agricultural future. While we are committed to producing high-value crops, our core mission is to develop and test cutting-edge agricultural technologies that will revolutionize farming practices across Tanzania and the African continent.

Our Unique Model

KIK Farms operates on a self-sustaining innovation model, where revenue from crop production funds research and development for locally built smart farming technologies. By owning and refining our innovations, we empower local farmers with affordable, high-impact solutions tailored to African conditions. This model ensures long-term sustainability, increased productivity, and improved livelihoods for farmers. Our journey begins in Dodoma, with plans to expand across Tanzania and beyond.

KIK Farms represents the new generation of farms designed for today and the future of agriculture. It is a smart, technology-driven farming concept aimed at revolutionizing food production, sustainability, and agribusiness development. With advanced agricultural techniques, digital tools, and data-driven decision-making, KIK Farms sets a benchmark for modern farming practices across Africa.
